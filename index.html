About Me

I'm your Technical Level Artist! working toward horror as a specialty. I'd like to build amazing levels that will leave the player amazed and make them remember the experience.

My strongest ground is Environment Art: dressing, modularity, material and texture work that reads as a real, inhabited (or once-inhabited) space. I pair that with Level Design, focusing on pacing, readability, and encounter and exploration flow. I'm developing my Lighting and Shader/Technical Art skills to communicate visual intent clearly and help the team bring a scene's mood to life.

I've pitched a project to publishers in private settings during DevGamm 2025 in Lisbon, gained experience leading artists, and trained myself to think quickly and efficiently during short game jams. What stays constant is my care for the aesthetic side of a level: how it's built, how it performs, and how it delivers the best possible experience for our players.
