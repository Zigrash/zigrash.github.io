About

I'm a Technical Level Artist working toward horror as a specialty — the genre where environment art and level design have to carry the story, because nothing else is doing the talking for you.

My strongest ground is Environment Art: dressing, modularity, material and texture work that reads as a real, inhabited (or once-inhabited) space. I pair that with solid Level Design — pacing, readability, and encounter/exploration flow — and I'm building up my Lighting and shader/technical art skills so I can own more of a scene's mood myself rather than handing it off.

I've pitched a project on a public stage, led an art department, and shipped things in two weeks and in two years. What stays constant is caring about the technical side of a level: how it's built, how it performs, and how it can still scare someone.
